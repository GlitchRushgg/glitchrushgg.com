About

GlitchRushGG is a one-person indie studio making games that respect your time: open a link and you're playing, on the phone in your pocket or the laptop in front of you. No downloads, no stores, no accounts — just quick, colorful arcade fun built for those spare minutes.

The studio builds in public: every game ships free on the web, gets improved with player feedback, and new releases arrive every few weeks. The catalog spans one-button runners, survivor arenas, drop-and-merge puzzles, idle builders and cozy sandboxes.

History

GlitchRushGG started in 2026 with Don't Drown, Noah!, a vertical platformer about escaping a rising flood, and has since grown to ten games — each one small, finished and free. The studio's philosophy: ship small, listen to players, keep what's fun and build the next thing better.
